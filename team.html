Every one a volunteer

Nobody at Freedom Gathering gets paid. They show up because God put these people on their hearts.

Tonia Johnson

Founder

Tonia has a heart for God and for people. She started Freedom Gathering with Donny Johnson in 2014, and her desire has always been to see souls saved and to give food, nutrition and love to those in need.

Cathy Wilson

Ministry Assistant

Cathy keeps the ministry running behind the scenes, from the schedule to the partners to the people.

Ivory

Official unofficial mascot

Ivory the sheep is a picture of the Shepherd who leads and tends His people. She comforts the ones who need a little extra attention, brings smiles to the kids, and never turns down a treat.
