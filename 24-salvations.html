24 salvations in one Wednesday

On Wednesday, October 28, twenty-four people gave their lives to Jesus at our outreach in Bradenton.

Twenty-four. In one afternoon. Under a tent, next to a lunch line, in a parking lot.

That is why we do this. The plates matter. The clothes matter. But a hot meal lasts a few hours, and Jesus lasts forever. Every Wednesday we preach the gospel and pray with anyone who wants prayer, and on October 28 God moved.

What a Wednesday looks like

Lunch starts at noon and runs until 3:00. Volunteers serve home-cooked plates, hand out clothes, and pass out water. We even bring dog food and cat food, because a lot of our friends have pets they love, and they get hungry too.

Then we pray. We preach. And we watch God do what only He can do.

Come see for yourself

Every Wednesday from 12:00 to 3:00 at 1701 14th Street W in Bradenton. Come serve, come pray, or come eat. There is a seat for you.
