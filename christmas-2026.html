Christmas is coming

For a lot of the families we serve, Christmas does not happen unless somebody makes it happen. So every December, we throw two parties.

Manatee County

On the first Saturday in December, we host a Christmas party in Manatee County for families who might not otherwise have Christmas.

Arcadia

In Arcadia, we put on a big Christmas party and gift giveaway.

How you can help

Gifts, food, volunteers and prayer. Every one of them matters. Sign up to serve, or give toward Christmas.
