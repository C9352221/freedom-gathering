How it started

A grandson, a gallon of water, and some cups.

For years, Tonia Johnson drove past the homeless along US-41 on her way to work. Then her grandson took his recess money to the dollar store, bought a gallon of water and some cups, and started handing it out.

It began in the parking lot of her furniture store with 30 people. Twelve years later, Freedom Gathering serves 300 to 500 people every week across Manatee County, and not one person on the team is paid.

Coming to a neighborhood near you

Fill the Table. Fill the Neighborhood.

Our new two hour pop-up food distributions go straight into the neighborhoods where groceries are hardest to come by. We park, we set up, and we fill as many tables as we can.

Every week we already carry fresh produce to five trailer parks. The pop-ups take that further.
