About us

The Heartbeat of God

Freedom Gathering leads people to Jesus and shows His love by meeting real needs, one plate, one prayer, and one neighborhood at a time.

How it started

It started with a boy and his recess money.

For years, Tonia Johnson drove past the homeless along US-41 and kept going. Her grandson did not. He took his recess money to the dollar store, bought a gallon of water and some cups, and started handing out water to anyone who was thirsty.

That was enough to stop her. In 2014, Donny and Tonia Johnson opened up the parking lot of her furniture store, and 30 people came.

Today Freedom Gathering serves 300 to 500 people every week, seven days a week, across Manatee County. Every single person on the team is a volunteer. Nobody is paid.

Our mission

To share the love of Christ through food, compassion, and consistent outreach to the homeless and hurting in our community, meeting both physical and spiritual needs with the heart of Jesus.

We believe every person is valuable, seen, and loved by God, and we exist to remind them of that truth.

Partners

None of this happens alone

Through Good Neighbors, local restaurants and grocers give us the food that ends up on our tables. Other partners come alongside us to care for the whole person.

Food Partners

Sprouts, Trader Joe's, Olive Garden, Longhorn Steakhouse, Seasons 52, Yard House, IMG Academy and McDonald's, with Whole Foods coming soon. We also shop the Manatee County Food Bank.

Care Partners

Centerstone sets up at our Wednesday outreach every other week with help for mental health, addiction, recovery and crisis. Manatee County Health comes once a month with health services and help getting prescriptions. We also work with the Salvation Army of Bradenton and Manatee County.

Where the Food Goes

Loving Hands Ministries, The Crossings Ministry, the residents of Christian Retreat, Rainbow Trailer Park, Manatee Trailer Park, Two Rivers Mobile Home Park, North River Care, and our Wednesday lunch.
